What sponsors receive

  • Early access to new featuresTry new skills and web workspace features before they are publicly released
  • A say in the roadmapFeatures you propose are taken into account when we set development priorities
  • Dedicated support channelWe open a separate channel for installation, connection and operations questions
  • Adoption and training sessionsWe run adoption briefings and usage training for your in-house teams
  • Priority PoC supportWe set up a web workspace PoC in your environment first
  • Sponsor recognitionIf you wish, we list you as a sponsor on our website and GitHub repository

What we can do together through Co-work

Custom skill development
We design and build skills together that fit your business procedures and deliverable templates.
Industry and module focus
We refine analysis and documentation together for industry-specific processes, such as manufacturing, distribution or finance, or for particular modules.
Joint PoC
We run part of a real project with SC4SAP and measure its impact and limits together.
Open-source contributions
You can take part directly in the plug-in repository through issues and pull requests.
